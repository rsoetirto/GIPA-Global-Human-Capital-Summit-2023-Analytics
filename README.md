# Global Human Capital Summit 2023

### Overview ### 
The Global Human Capital Summit 2023 is the flagship event of the Global Indonesia Professionals' Association (GIPA), an alliance representing Indonesian professionals across G20 and ASEAN nations. This annual event, held in Jakarta, serves as a key platform for fostering discussions on Indonesia’s human capital development, sovereignty, and economic progress, aligned with the vision of Indonesia Emas 2045.

---
### Key Highlights
- Attendance: Over 800 professionals, including global business leaders, government officials, and academics.
- Focus Areas: Green & Infrastructure, Public Sector, Health, Digital & Finance.
- Supporting Bodies: Ministry of Foreign Affairs, ASEAN Business Advisory Council.
- Notable Speakers: Dr. Kao Kim Hourn (Secretary General of ASEAN), Dharsono Hartono (Chairman of KADIN Indonesia Net Zero Hub), and Ambassador Siti Mauludiah (Senior Advisor to the Foreign Minister).
